01 / About

Engineering systems for clearer biological signals.

I’m a biomedical engineering researcher at UC Davis working where microfluidics, immunology, and quantitative analysis meet. I’m especially interested in tools that can isolate rare cell populations without losing the biological differences that make them valuable.

My current work uses controlled shear and antigen-functionalized microchannels to study and enrich B cells by binding avidity. I also design modular hardware, automated image-analysis pipelines, and experiments that can scale from proof of concept to primary samples.

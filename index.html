01 / About

Hey there! I’m Stephen. Thanks for stopping by!

I’m a bioengineer who designs microfluidic systems to explore biological questions. I especially enjoy taking an idea from an early sketch to a working experiment, with thoughtful design and rigorous science guiding each step. I’m happiest where good engineering meets a question worth answering. That’s where we can learn more about ourselves and the world around us, and perhaps make one small part of it better.

Right now, I’m a graduate researcher in the George Lab at UC Davis. My project uses controlled shear and functionalized microchannels to study and enrich B cells based on binding avidity. Along the way, I’ve developed modular, plug-and-play hardware, built automated image-analysis pipelines, and taken experiments from early models to studies with primary samples. The project has also given me plenty to share, including this poster and this video.

My path here has been anything but linear. I’ve worked in settings ranging from the back of an ambulance to student-led startups and research labs. Each stop has changed how I approach problem-solving and left me with a few good stories. Ask me about them!

Outside the lab, you can usually find me climbing 🧗, swimming 🏊, hanging out with my partner 👫, or playing social deduction games 🐺.
